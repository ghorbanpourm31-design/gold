# ConsultAxis website: technology, service and cookie inventory

Prepared for the legal review of the Imprint and Privacy Policy (legal brief, sections 5, 7, 14 and 16).
It describes the website **as built in this repository**. Each item was checked in a real browser (Chromium) and on a local PHP server. If anything is added later (analytics, maps, video, newsletter, booking, chat or CRM), update this file and the Privacy Policy **before** it goes live.

Status: 5 November 2026 · Build: `consultaxis/` · Languages: German (primary), English

| Question from the legal brief | Answer for this website |

## 4. Consent management

Built in and self-hosted (`src/assets/js/consent.js`, configured in `site.config.mjs → consent`).

- **Now:** no optional services are registered, so no banner is shown and nothing is stored. The footer link "Cookie-Einstellungen / Cookie settings" on every page opens a dialog explaining that no cookies are used.
- **When an optional service is registered** (for example self-hosted Matomo), the manager:
  - shows a dialog on the first visit with **equal "Reject all" and "Accept all" buttons** (same style and size, verified) plus per-category settings;
  - keeps every optional category **off by default**;
  - loads the service's script **only after consent** (verified: not loaded before consent or after rejection, loaded after acceptance);
  - records the choice with **policy version and timestamp**; raising `consent.version` asks everyone again;
  - lets visitors **change or withdraw** consent at any time via the footer link (withdrawal reloads the page so the service is unloaded).
- Not included: server-side consent logging. If the legal review requires an audit trail beyond the browser record, that needs a small server endpoint or a consent platform, and would itself have to be described in the Privacy Policy.

## 6. Contact form: data flow

1. Fields: **Name\***, **Email\***, **Area of interest\***, **Message\***, Company (optional), Telephone (optional). Required fields are marked. There is **no** "I agree to the Privacy Policy" checkbox and no marketing opt-in. A short privacy notice with a link to the Privacy Policy sits next to the submit button (wording from the legal brief, section 6).
2. Sent over HTTPS to `/api/contact.php` on our own server. Works with or without JavaScript.
3. The script checks: same-origin request, honeypot field empty, minimum fill time (3 s), field formats and lengths. Line breaks are stripped from header values, so header injection is not possible (tested).
4. It sends **one plain-text email** to info@consultaxis.de, with the visitor's address as Reply-To.
5. **Nothing is stored on the web server**: no database, no file, no log of the content. The host's mail log may record sender, recipient and time of the outgoing message; check this with the host.
6. Retention afterwards happens in the mailbox. **The client must define a deletion rule** (placeholder in the Privacy Policy, section 5).

Spam protection processes no extra personal data and needs no consent.

## 7. Hosting requirements and server logs

- Apache with `mod_rewrite` and `mod_headers` (for `.htaccess`), **PHP 8.0+** with `mail()` enabled, HTTPS. Any standard German shared-hosting package meets this. nginx works too (see README).
- Choose a host with servers in Germany/EU and sign its DPA.
- Ask the host for the **log retention period** and, if possible, IP anonymisation; enter the days in `site.config.mjs → hosting.logRetentionDays`.
- Backups: confirm the backup location with the host (EU).

## 8. Security measures (implemented)

HTTPS enforced with HSTS; strict Content-Security-Policy (`'self'` only); `X-Frame-Options: DENY`; `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`; `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`; Permissions-Policy disabling camera, microphone, geolocation and payment; no directory listings; the generated form configuration is not reachable from the web (tested: 403); server-side validation of every form field; same-origin check on the form endpoint (tested: 403 for foreign origins).

5. **Data Protection Officer:** required or not (Privacy Policy, section 2).
6. **Competent supervisory authority** for the company's registered seat (Privacy Policy, section 12).
7. **Wording on regulated activities:** the site states that ConsultAxis does not act as an estate agent (§ 34c GewO) and does not provide legal or tax advice. Please confirm this wording, and the boundary list on the Real Estate page.
8. **Client wording to double-check:** the German service list includes "Wirtschaftliche Objektbewertungen und Marktpreisorientierung". Because certified valuation reports are excluded, consider whether "Objekteinschätzungen" fits better. Unchanged pending your decision.
9. **Partners page:** shows partner *areas* only and says that individual partners will be introduced once confirmed.
10. **Accessibility (BFSG):** built with semantic headings, a skip link, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled form fields with error messages, alternative text, `prefers-reduced-motion` support and measured contrast (body text 13–14:1; gold text only on navy at 5.35:1; darker gold #7A6238 on light backgrounds at 5.3:1). Whether the BFSG applies still needs an assessment.
