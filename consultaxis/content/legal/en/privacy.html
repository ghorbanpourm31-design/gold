3. Overview

This website is deliberately lean. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage in your browser, uses no analytics, tracking or marketing services, no social media plugins, and embeds no maps, videos or other third-party content. Fonts, images, icons and scripts are loaded exclusively from our own web server.

Personal data is processed only in the following cases:

  • when you visit the website (server log files, section 4),
  • when you write to us using the contact form (section 5),
  • when you contact us by email (section 6).

4. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by {{hosting.provider}}. The servers are located in {{hosting.serverLocation}}. The connection to the website is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS).

With every request, your browser technically transmits information to the server, which is stored in log files (server log files):

  • IP address of the requesting device,
  • date and time of access,
  • requested page or file and the amount of data transferred,
  • whether the request was successful (HTTP status code),
  • previously visited page (referrer URL), if transmitted,
  • browser type and version, and operating system.

Purpose: delivering the website, ensuring its stability and security, and detecting and preventing attacks.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable provision of the website.

Retention: log files are deleted after {{hosting.logRetentionDays}} days unless further retention is required to investigate a specific security incident.

Recipients: the hosting provider processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.
