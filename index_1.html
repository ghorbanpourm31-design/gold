Gold, struck in your name

Gold starts
in the dark.

Follow one thread of it out of the rock, through fire and proof, into a vault. It ends as a bar with your initials on it.

Up to three letters.

At 1,064 °C it lets go of everything that is not gold.

Melted once. Cast once.

Then it is proved.

Weight, fineness, serial. Written down, then struck into the metal.

Then it is kept.

Held in a vault, allocated to you by serial number, and yours to sell or collect.

This one is yours.
